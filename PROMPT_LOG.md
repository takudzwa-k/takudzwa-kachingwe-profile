# Prompt Log — About Section Copy

## 1. My Prompt

> **Context:** I'm a second-year Software Engineering student at Africa University, building a one-page personal
> profile site for a Web Technologies assignment.
> Write a short "About Me" bio (3–4 sentences) for the page.
> Keep it under 60 words, friendly but professional tone, avoid generic buzzwords like "passionate"
> or "innovative," and mention that I'm still learning front-end development and interested in AI-assisted design.
> Plain paragraph text, no headings, no bullet points.

## 2. AI's Raw Output

> "I am a passionate and innovative second-year Software Engineering student at Africa University with a strong
> drive to become an outstanding developer. I am deeply committed to mastering front-end development and am
> constantly exploring cutting-edge tools, including AI-assisted design, to build innovative and impactful digital
> solutions that make a real difference in the world."

## 3. My Final Edited Version

> "I'm a second-year Software Engineering student at Africa University with a growing interest in front-end
> development and user-centred design. I enjoy turning ideas into working interfaces and I'm currently sharpening
> my HTML, CSS, and JavaScript fundamentals while exploring how AI tools can speed up good
> design thinking."

## 4. Reflection

The raw output ignored my constraint about avoiding buzzwords — it used "passionate" and "innovative" twice each
and ran well over the 60-word limit, so it didn't actually sound like me. I cut the inflated claims ("outstanding
developer," "impactful digital solutions"), replaced them with specific, true details (the actual languages I'm
learning), and added the line about AI "speeding up, not replacing" design thinking, since that's a nuance I
actually believe and wanted the reader to see. The edited version is shorter, more concrete, and reads like
something I'd actually say about myself rather than generic marketing copy.
